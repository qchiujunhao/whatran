One finalization pass

Freedom while working. Structure when done.

Whatran does not record the agent’s clicks or failed attempts. It captures only the paths that support the reported result, then gives those paths an executable boundary.

01

Finalize

Separate raw inputs from generated files and turn every retained operation into an ordered script.

02

Rerun

Rebuild the declared environment and regenerate results through one canonical command.

03

Check

A fresh CI clone moves the references aside, reruns the analysis, and compares outputs.

04

Show

Publish the finding, figures, final scripts, rerun command, and exact verification boundary.

What the green check means
  • The repository was checked out fresh.
  • Committed results were moved away.
  • The final pipeline regenerated every declared output.
  • Regenerated outputs matched the references under declared rules.

It does not certify scientific correctness or prove that no relevant decision was omitted.

Method, not trace

Keep what another person needs.

The deliverable is a normal repository, not a proprietary capsule. A human can inspect it; another coding agent can take it over.

Whatran keeps

  • Declared raw inputs and environment
  • Final result-affecting scripts
  • Parameters, branches, and comparison rules
  • Reference results and generated figures
  • Rerun instructions and verification evidence

Whatran leaves behind

  • Chat history and hidden reasoning
  • Debugging commands and failed attempts
  • Discarded branches that are not reported
  • Temporary files, caches, and notebook state
  • Claims the outputs cannot support
